GF LOVE WEBSITE
================

1. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js and the images folder together.
2. Open index.html in Chrome.
3. Your 12 uploaded photos are already included.
4. To add music:
   - create a folder named music
   - put your song there as music.mp3
   - uncomment the music section at the bottom of script.js

Easy customizations:
- Change romantic text directly in index.html.
- Change photo captions in script.js.
- Colors and design are in style.css.
